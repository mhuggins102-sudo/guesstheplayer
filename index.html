Guess The Player

MLB Edition

    Practice Stats

    How to Play

    Guess the mystery MLB player by comparing career stats!

    Getting Started

    Type a player name in the search bar and select from the suggestions. Each guess reveals how that player compares to the mystery player.

    Cell Colors

    Green — Exact match
    Yellow — Close (within threshold)
    Red — Far off
    Gray — N/A (wrong player type)

    Arrows

    ↑ means the mystery player's value is higher. ↓ means it's lower.

    Name Highlighting

    First or last name initials that match the mystery player are highlighted in green.

    Tap for Details

    • Player name — opens a full stat card
    • Column headers — shows the narrowed-down range based on your guesses
    • Teams cell — shows which teams the guessed player played for

    Hints

    After 4 guesses, a Hint button appears. Each hint reveals a specific stat value for the mystery player. Use them wisely!

    Game Modes

    • Practice — Play unlimited games. Choose your era and difficulty.
    • Daily — One puzzle per day, same for everyone. Era and difficulty are locked.
