Guess The Player

MLB Edition

    Practice Stats

    How to Play

    Guess the mystery MLB player by comparing career stats!

    Game Modes

    • Practice — Play unlimited games. Choose your era and difficulty.
    • Daily — One puzzle per day, same for everyone. Era and difficulty are locked.

    Player Pool

    • Era — Narrow the pool by when players were active: All-Time, 1940+, 1960+, 1980+, 2000+ (default), or Active (currently playing).
    • Difficulty — Control how well-known the mystery player is: Easy includes only the most famous players, Medium adds recognizable names, and Hard includes the full pool.

    Gameplay

    From your selected era, type a player name in the search bar. Each guess reveals how that player compares to the mystery player. Use the clues to keep making guesses until your find the mystery player!

    Cell Colors

    Green — Exact match
    Yellow — Close (see Categories section)
    Red — Far off
    Gray — N/A (wrong player type)

    Arrows

    ↑ means the mystery player's value is higher.
    ↓ means it's lower.

    Name Highlighting

    First or last name initials that match the mystery player are highlighted in green.

    Added Details

    Hover or tap on the following to reveal additional information:

    • Player name — opens a full stat card
    • Column headers — shows the narrowed-down range based on your guesses
    • Teams cell — shows which teams the guessed player played for

    Hints

    After 4 guesses, a Hint button appears. Each hint reveals a specific stat value for the mystery player. A new hint will be available after each guess.
